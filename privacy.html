Privacy, Phone, and Text Message Policy

Who We Are

Our website address is https://yesformentalhealth.vote. This site is published by Citizens for a Healthy Community.

What This Website Collects

This website is a simple informational site. It has no user accounts, no comment forms, and sets no cookies of its own. We do not collect personal information through this website.

Embedded Content and Outside Links

Pages on this site may link to or embed content from other websites (for example, our donation page or published reports). When you follow those links, the other website's own privacy practices apply. Those websites may collect data about you, use cookies, and monitor your interaction with their content.

Phone Number and Text Message Policy

Citizens for a Healthy Community respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. We collect your name, email address, and cell phone number when you provide it to us. We use this information to send you campaign messages, provide support, and improve our outreach. We will keep your information for as long as you remain on our contact list, or until you ask us to delete it.

We will protect your information by using industry-standard security measures. We will not share your information with third parties without your consent. However, we may share your information with third-party service providers who help us operate our website and deliver our messages. These service providers are required to keep your information confidential and to use it only for the purposes for which we have provided it to them.

Opting Out

You can opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying “STOP” to any text message we send you, or by clicking the “unsubscribe” link at the bottom of any email we send you.

Your Rights

You have the right to access your personal information and to request that we correct any inaccuracies. You also have the right to request that we delete your information. To exercise these rights, please contact us at dquiote@att.net.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make any significant changes, we will post a notice on this website.

Questions

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at dquiote@att.net.
